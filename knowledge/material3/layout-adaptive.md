# Material layout and adaptive design

The Material sitemap lists layout structure, adaptive design, breakpoints, grids/spacing, density, scaffold bars/panes/rails, canonical feed/list-detail/supporting-pane examples, and RTL. Their page bodies were not reviewed; consult the relevant official page before relying on a Material-specific rule.

For Compose implementation, use current Android adaptive-layout guidance and the target app's window-size/navigation approach. Test representative window sizes, resizing, insets, and directionality. Material layout patterns describe design intent; AndroidX APIs and app constraints determine the implementation.

**Provenance:** `material3_site` (sitemap-listed paths); `androidx_compose_adaptive`.
