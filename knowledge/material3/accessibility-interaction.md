# Material accessibility and interaction

The Material sitemap exposes foundations for assistive technology, interaction inputs/gestures/selection/state layers, text resizing/truncation, color contrast, and per-component accessibility pages. Their article bodies are not verified here; retrieve the relevant pages before asserting their exact recommendations.

For Compose, use Android accessibility guidance and semantic APIs. Check labels/roles, focus order, touch/input alternatives, contrast for approved colors, font scaling, state announcement, and reduced-motion behavior as appropriate to the feature. Automated checks complement manual accessibility review; they do not establish full conformance.
