# Cards (View library)

The guide describes elevated, filled, and outlined MaterialCardView variants, lists default/checked/dragged states, and says checked behavior must be supplied rather than assumed. It documents View state-list styling and a View AccessibilityDelegate example. This is a component-library reference, not a Material.io design page or Compose card contract.

## Scope and implementation boundary
