# Navigation rail (View library)

The page describes collapsed/expanded View navigation rails, says the expanded form replaces the drawer in its Expressive update, and documents a transition between forms. It identifies menu titles for screen-reader announcements and label-visibility modes. The examples use NavigationRailView, menu XML, and View transitions; the page does not establish Compose NavigationSuiteScaffold or other AndroidX APIs.
